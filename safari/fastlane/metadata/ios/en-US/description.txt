DEVICE GPS (PRO · EXPERIMENTAL)

Go beyond the browser: align your iPhone's system location to the place you choose, so location-aware apps — not just Safari — see it too. This optional Pro feature requires the free GeoSpoof GPS companion app for Mac: a one-time setup over a cable, then it works over Wi-Fi. It uses Apple's standard developer location tools and never modifies iOS.

WHAT GEOSPOOF DOES NOT DO

GeoSpoof is not a VPN and does not change your IP address. For full location privacy, use it alongside a VPN. It does not change your browser's language or locale, bypass server-side detection (IP, payment methods, account history), or collect any data about you or your browsing.
